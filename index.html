Frequently Asked

Answer before the client has to call

Do I need a consultation before hair colour?

A consultation helps assess the starting condition, previous colour, desired result, maintenance and whether a strand test is appropriate.

Should bridal makeup include a trial?

A trial is useful for discussing skin, outfit, jewellery, photography conditions, product preferences and the wedding-day timeline.

Are service prices final?

The concept menu is illustrative. A real salon should clearly explain when hair length, product use, complexity or add-ons change the price.

Are the specialists and results shown here real?

No. This is a portfolio concept. Replace names, qualifications, photographs, prices and transformations with verified client content.
